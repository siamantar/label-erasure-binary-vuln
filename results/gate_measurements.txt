INTERPRETATION AS RECORDED

  The transformer saturates the GPU at 98 to 99 percent while its clock sits
  around 1.9 GHz, so it is compute-bound. The BiGRU idles at roughly half
  utilization while holding 2.75 GHz, so the recurrence is launch-bound: the
  per-timestep kernels are too small to fill the device and the sequential
  dependency prevents overlap.
